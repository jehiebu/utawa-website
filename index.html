Supporting Animal Welfare at U of T

University of Toronto Animal Welfare Association brings together students who care about animals and want to make a difference. Through education, advocacy, and community initiatives, we promote compassion, responsibility, and better welfare for animals.

About Us

The University of Toronto Animal Welfare Association (UTAWA) is a student-led organization dedicated to promoting animal welfare, raising awareness, and encouraging meaningful action within our community. We bring together students who share a passion for animals and believe that even small actions can make a difference.

Beyond education and advocacy, we work with and maintain connections with local animal rescue organizations to support animal welfare efforts in the Greater Toronto Area. Through these partnerships, we aim to provide students with opportunities to get involved in volunteering, animal rescue initiatives, fostering, and promoting responsible pet ownership and adoption.

We also strive to create a welcoming and supportive community where students can share experiences, exchange knowledge, and explore issues related to animal welfare. Through educational events, community outreach, and collaborative projects, we hope to bridge the gap between students and organizations working directly to improve animals’ lives.

At UTAWA, we believe that compassion should go beyond awareness and lead to meaningful action. Whether you have years of experience working with animals or simply want to learn how to help, everyone is welcome to be part of our community.

What We Do

Animal Rescue & Volunteering

Collaborate with local shelters and rescue organizations to provide students with opportunities to support animals in need.

Fostering & Adoption

Promote responsible adoption and foster care while helping rescue animals find loving homes.

Fundraising & Donations

Organize fundraising events and donation drives to support local animal rescue organizations.

Education & Workshops

Host workshops and guest speakers to share knowledge about animal welfare, pet care, and veterinary medicine.

Awareness & Advocacy

Raise awareness about animal welfare issues through campus events and social media campaigns.

Community Events

Bring together students who love animals through social gatherings and interactive activities.
